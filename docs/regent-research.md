## 1. Core mechanics

- **Start of run:** 75 HP. Deck: 4 Strike, 4 Defend, Falling Star, Venerate. Starter relic **Divine Right**: gain 3★ at the start of each combat [W-Regent, UC]. Strike = 6 dmg, Defend = 5 Block [J page card text].
- **Stars:** a second resource alongside energy. Stars don't reset at end of turn and have no cap [UG]. Some cards cost ★ in addition to (or instead of) energy [MG]. Divine Right's 3★ count as "gained this turn" on turn 1 (so Radiate hits for 9 on turn 1) [J, B].
- **Starter economy:** a fight starts with 3★, and each Venerate play adds 2★ (3★ upgraded). Falling Star costs 2★. So the starter deck supports about one star spender per deck cycle. Astral Pulse (3★), for example, may not be castable on the second cycle unless Venerate is drawn first [UG]. Venerate costs 1E [UC].
- **Falling Star:** 0E + 2★. Deal 8 (12) dmg, apply 1 Weak and 1 Vulnerable [W-FS, UC]. Archaic Tooth (from the ancient Orobas at the start of Act 2) transforms it into **Meteor Shower**: 0E + 2★, 14 (21) to ALL enemies, 2 Weak + 2 Vulnerable to ALL [W-Archaic Tooth, UC].
- **Forge / Sovereign Blade:** the first Forge in a combat adds a Sovereign Blade to your hand. Each later Forge adds its amount to every Blade [W-SB, MG]. Blade: 2E, 10 dmg, Retain; upgraded costs 1E. It exists only for that combat and never enters your deck. If it is exhausted or transformed, the next Forge creates a fresh Blade without the earlier Forge damage. Upgrades to it do last for the rest of that combat [W-SB]. The Blade counts as a Colorless card, so it works with Heirloom Hammer, Arsenal and similar cards [J, B].
- **Third axis, card creation / Colorless:** CHARGE!!, BEGONE! and GUARDS!!! transform cards into Minion cards. Crash Landing and Collision Course add Debris. Spectrum Shift, Quasar, Manifest Authority, Bundle of Joy and Orange Dough create Colorless cards. Payoffs are Pillar of Creation, Arsenal and Supermassive [MG, CG]. MG: "Regent lacks good on demand draw, but makes up for it with card creation."
- **Starter weaknesses:** the starter deck leans too far toward damage (remove 1–2 Strikes before any Defend) [J]. Two starter cards give no Block, so hands with no Block card are common; add a dense Block card [MG].
- **Ascension facts that matter** [W-Asc]: A1 has about 60% more elites. A2: Ancients heal only 80%. A6: card removal costs 100 gold, then +50 each time. A7: rares and upgraded cards appear half as often. A8: enemies have more HP. A9: enemies deal more damage. **A10: two bosses at the end of Act 3.** NGA2 (A10): at A9+ the hardest point is the first boss; with mediocre early cards, a loose deck ends up short on damage or one energy short.

## 4. Card-reward, deck, upgrade and relic heuristics

### By act (A7+ data [U] plus expert comments)
- **Act 1 (survive the first boss; the main wall at A9+ [NGA2]).** Take front-loaded damage, AoE and dense Block. Best A1 run-win deltas: Big Bang +9.1, Bombardment +7.5, Tyranny +6.4, Bundle of Joy +4.5, Comet +4.2, Make It So +4.2, Manifest Authority +4.1, GUARDS!!! +3.9, Gamma Blast +3.8, Arsenal +3.5, Spectrum Shift +3.4, Bulwark +3.4, Pillar +3.3 [U]. Act 1 "bridge" cards that are fine now but bad later: Kingly Punch/Kick, Shining Strike (+6.7 act delta), Wrought in War, Refine Blade, Heirloom Hammer (+6.8 act delta) [U]. Early commons worth taking per MG: Astral Pulse, Collision Course, CHARGE!!, Crash Landing, Glitterstream, Bulwark, Know Thy Place.
- **Skip in Act 1** (A1 run delta ≤ −3): Monologue, Resonance, Terraforming, Crescent Spear, Hammer Time, Seven Stars, Beat into Shape, Alignment, The Smith, Monarch's Gaze, Royalties, Devastate [U]. B does rate Royalties B (30 gold per fight pays for removals).
- **Act 2.** Shift to scaling engines. Positive: Big Bang +5.4, Prophesize +4.4, Pale Blue Dot +4.2, Tyranny +3.8, Pillar +3.4, Spectrum Shift +3.3, Arsenal +2.7, Bundle of Joy +2.6, Gamma Blast +2.6, GUARDS!!! +2.6, Crash Landing +2.5 [U]. **Stop taking** Kingly Punch (−10.5), Kingly Kick (−9.8), Wrought in War (−9.8), Solar Strike (−9.5), Crush Under (−9.3), Photon Cut (−8.4), Astral Pulse (−7.4), Patter, Shining Strike, The Smith (all about −7) [U].
- **Act 3 (A10 means two bosses [W-Asc]).** Positive: Spectrum Shift +8.4, Bundle of Joy +7.6, Pillar +7.5, Pale Blue Dot +6.2, Tyranny +5.4, Arsenal +3.5, Big Bang +3.1, Spoils of Battle +2.9, Quasar +2.8, Manifest Authority +2.6, Supermassive +2.5 [U]. Avoid: Hegemony (−13.9), Kingly Punch/Kick, Shining Strike, Solar Strike, Astral Pulse, Wrought in War (all −10 to −12), Cloak of Stars (−10.1), Crescent Spear (−10.1), Gather Light (−9.5), Patter, Devastate, Conqueror, The Smith (about −9) [U]. J makes the exception that Know Thy Place can be better in Act 3 than in Act 1.
- **How to read the data:** the deltas compare picking with not picking when offered. They are confounded by deck strength and compressed for cards everyone takes (Reflect: 74% picked in A1, +1.4). Treat them as a check on expert opinion, not as ground truth. Sample sizes of cards added in v0.108–v0.109 (Plot, Constellation, Tutor) are about 1/6 of their peers', which suggests the window spans several patches (my inference).

### Star budget (pick-time rule)
- Count ★ produced per deck cycle against ★ costs in the deck. Too many star cards means strong cards you can't reliably play [UG]. After subtracting the energy spent on star cards, the rest is your usable energy [NGA2].
- Don't reflexively take a second Glow; first check it doesn't starve energy or delay key cards [NGA2]. Take star generators early once you have payoffs, and accept payoffs rather than waiting for a perfect setup [N1G]. Jorbs' view: Hidden Cache is "the default sensible way" to get Stars [J]. JE's view: add Hidden Cache only when your existing payoffs need more fuel [JE].
- The star payoffs compete: Gamma Blast competes with Reflect and Comet for the same ★. Know Thy Place gives the same debuffs without spending ★ [JE, B].

### Deck size and removal
- Remove Strikes first: 1–2 Strikes before any Defend [J]. B rates Strike F ("one of your best remove targets") and says upgraded Defends stay useful [B]. At A6+ each removal costs +50 gold, so each one is precious [W-Asc].
- In-combat thinning replaces shop removals: CHARGE!!, GUARDS!!!, BEGONE! and Tyranny can exhaust curses, statuses and Strikes [B, J, JE]. B and ST1 treat the Minion cards as thinning tools.
- Keep the deck lean and take only cards that solve current problems [N1G]. NGA2 dissents: don't remove blindly early, and pick removals by attack/defense balance.
- Pathing: Act 1 favors fires and hallway fights, with elites late [N1G]. A weak Regent dodges Act 1 elites and uses shops and fights to see cards [NGA1].

### Upgrade priorities
- **Recommended upgrades:** J flags Arsenal (Innate), Make It So, Tyranny (Innate), Glitterstream, Kingly Punch, Royal Gamble (adds Retain), Venerate, Crush Under, Pale Blue Dot, Stardust [J]. B flags The Sealed Throne (0E), Tyranny, Bombardment, Meteor Shower, Crash Landing, Astral Pulse, Hidden Cache (next-turn 3★→4★), Orbit (2E→1E), Pale Blue Dot ("must-upgrade", draw 2), Spectrum Shift (2E→1E), Quasar, Convergence (2★ next turn), Glow (2★), Sword Sage (2E→1E) and more [B, UC].
- JE: Glow+ is "a high priority", because 2★ from Glow lets you play Falling Star and still hold 3★ for Reflect or Gamma Blast [MG, JE]. Know Thy Place+ loses Exhaust. Spectrum Shift+ and Orbit+ lower setup cost. Big Bang's Innate upgrade is rarely urgent [JE].
- N1G upgrade order for Act 1: star generation (if you have payoffs) > cost cutters (Alignment, Orbit, Spectrum Shift) > Block (Gather Light, Particle Wall) > draw (Guiding Star, Pale Blue Dot) > damage (Astral Pulse, Gamma Blast) [N1G].
- **What A7+ players actually upgrade in Act 1:** Spectrum Shift 77%, Orbit 73%, Monarch's Gaze 68%, Sword Sage 65%, Bombardment 57%, Arsenal 48%, Manifest Authority 42%, Tyranny 37%, Quasar 37%, Bulwark 35%, Big Bang 34%. They almost never upgrade Falling Star (1%), Defend or Strike [U].
- The Blade can only be upgraded mid-combat, since it isn't in the deck [W-SB].

## 5. Combat heuristics (Regent-specific)

- **Banking stars is free.** Stars persist and have no cap [UG]. Keep a reserve when you hold, or will soon draw, an expensive payoff: Comet 5★, Decisions 6★, The Smith 4★, or Royal Gamble, which needs 5★ just to cast (it gives 9★) [JE]. Don't spend the starting ★ before the fight's important star attack [JE on Astral Pulse]. Avoid drawing Comet before you can afford it [JE].
- **Spend stars when:**
  - enemies attack big: Reflect gives 15 (20) Block and reflects blocked attack damage, and "Playing Reflect every turn sort of just wins the game on its own" [J, UC]
  - a big multi-hit or group turn is coming: Dying Star is Ethereal, deals 9 AoE and makes ALL enemies lose 9 Strength this turn, so it can be right to hold other star cards to afford it [UG, JE]
  - you have surplus ★: Particle Wall gives 9 Block for 2★ and returns to your hand, so you can repeat it; the ratio is poor, but it dumps excess ★ into Block [J, B]
  - you have Child of the Stars or Black Hole: spend every turn. Child gives 2 (3) Block per ★ spent. Black Hole fires on each gain or spend event, not per ★ [UC, JE]
- **Turn-1 tricks:** Radiate deals 9 AoE for 0E off Divine Right's 3★. Astral Pulse is affordable with the starting ★ [J, B].
- **Energy is the real bottleneck.** Budget energy spent on star generation against energy for Block and damage [NGA2]. Hold Spectrum Shift or other creation cards only if you keep energy to play what they create [JE].
- **Forging timing:**
  - Forge early on turns you must block anyway (Bulwark gives 12 Block + Forge 10). The Blade retains, so swing it on a turn when it is safe [J on Wrought in War] or when the enemy is Vulnerable, which Retain makes easy to line up [UG].
  - Conqueror is a finisher for an already-big Blade ("36 Damage for 1" with a 30-damage Blade) [J].
  - Summon Forth fetches the Blade for a second swing in the same turn [B, UG]. Seeking Edge turns the Blade into AoE [UC].
  - Refine Blade gives +1E next turn: play it ahead of a predictable danger turn [JE].
  - Don't overinvest against enemies that cap damage per hit [UG].
- **Debris and creation sequencing:**
  - Play Crash Landing (21 AoE; fills your hand with Debris) late in a fight or when your hand is already full [B, JE].
  - Collision Course's Debris often never needs playing if the fight ends first [MG].
  - Pillar of Creation turns created cards and Debris into Block [B]. GUARDS!!! Minion Sacrifices can be left unplayed and redrawn later [MG].
- **Bombardment:** play it early, then spend energy on Block while it recurs from the exhaust pile [MG, JE].
- **Shining Strike** puts itself on top of the draw pile, taking the next draw. Use it only when you want it again [B, JE].
- **Void Form** ends your turn when played. Plan a safe setup turn [UC, J]. Free plays don't count as spending ★ [JE].
- **Tyranny's exhaust is targeted:** use it on Strikes, statuses and curses (it even removes Bad Luck or Enthralled) [JE, B]. **CHARGE!!** needs cards left in the draw pile; target Strikes, curses and statuses [JE, B].
- **Debuffs:** Know Thy Place (0E) keeps Weak and Vulnerable up without spending ★; after upgrading it loses Exhaust, so recycle it [JE, B]. J: Know Thy Place is exceptional in excess-draw decks that don't yet keep enemies permanently debuffed.
- **Kingly Kick** gets 1E cheaper each draw. Pay 3–4E on the first draw when the damage is needed; top-deck manipulation speeds it up [J, B, MG].
- **Boss notes [N1G, 2026-05-28]:** Vantom and Kin Shaman are hard (Kin Shaman needs AoE such as Astral Pulse or Black Hole). Soul Fysh and Lagavulin Matriarch are favorable. Multi-hit cards like Celestial Might are good into Vantom [B] and "helpful against hit-count defenses" [JE].
- **Common mistakes:**
  - too many star spenders for your generation, or the reverse [UG, ST1, NGA2]
  - delaying star generators, or taking payoffs too late [N1G]
  - forcing Arsenal-type payoffs without support [N1G]
  - over-picking Alignment [MG]
  - a reflexive second Glow [NGA2]
  - spreading picks across Stars and Forge. N1G (via its guide summary) says commit to one; UG says Forge works *alongside* Stars through cards that justify themselves (Bulwark). Treat Forge as a finisher, not a second engine [UG].
